Privacy Policy for Zogg

Effective Date: October 27, 2026

Welcome to Zogg ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you as to how we look after your personal data when you use our mobile application (Zogg) and tell you about your privacy rights.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the Zogg app, we may collect the following information:

  • Health & fitness data: Step counts, distance, estimated active calories, weight, water intake, and workout history. Records created in Zogg are stored locally on your device. If you connect Health Connect, Zogg reads daily step totals and can write completed workouts, distance, and estimated active calories as described on the Health Connect screen.
  • Location data: Location is accessed only while you actively record a GPS workout, to calculate and display its route. Workout route points are stored on your device.
  • Advertising data: Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, usage, and diagnostic data to provide and measure banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. If you choose to watch a rewarded ad, it may unlock optional bonus fitness content for 24 hours; the bonus is not unlocked unless the ad reward is earned.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

  • Providing and maintaining our step tracking and fitness services.
  • Displaying personal workout progress, streaks, and achievements.
  • Showing ads through Google AdMob. Rewarded ads are optional and can provide temporary access to bonus fitness content.

3. Data Security & Storage

Data entered or collected by Zogg is stored in the app's local database on your device. Zogg does not operate a server that receives your fitness records. Health Connect data is accessed or written only with your permission. You can export or delete local records in Settings; deleting local records does not delete data already written to Health Connect. Google, Health Connect, and AdMob process data under their own privacy policies.

4. Third-Party Services

Zogg uses Google AdMob to show ads. Google services may process data as described in their policies. Review the Google Privacy Policy and Google Ads Privacy Policy.

5. Your Choices

You can manage Health Connect permissions in Health Connect and export or delete Zogg's local data in Settings.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@zoggapp.com.
